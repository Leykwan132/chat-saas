# Services

## Overview

Services define the appointments KiloBot can offer. A Service combines the customer-facing description, timing rules, booking fields, sales style, and teammate assignment strategy used by the **Book appointment** Workflow action. Pair Services with **Availability** and review outcomes in **Calendar**.

## Create a Service

<ol className="steps">
  <li>Open <strong>Services</strong> and choose <strong>Add a service</strong>.</li>
  <li>Complete <strong>Details</strong> — enter the Service name, description, and active state.</li>
  <li>Complete <strong>Timing</strong> — set appointment duration, buffer, timezone, and optional preferred times.</li>
  <li>Complete <strong>Data collection</strong> — choose the fields KiloBot collects during booking.</li>
  <li>Complete <strong>Assignment</strong> — choose the sales style and teammate assignment strategy.</li>
  <li><strong>Save</strong> the Service.</li>
</ol>

Default booking fields include Booking Date, Booking Time, Customer Name, and Phone Number. You can add text, number, select, yes/no, date, time, and phone fields. Keep the form short enough for a customer to finish in conversation.

## Choose an assignment strategy

Assignment decides which teammate receives a booked appointment. Availability is still required — an assignment strategy does not make an unavailable teammate bookable.

- **Conversation owner first** prefers the teammate already responsible for the conversation.
- **Balanced** distributes bookings based on available workload.
- **Round robin** rotates assignment through eligible teammates.
- **Specific teammate** sends the Service to one selected person.

## Activate and connect the Service

Before customers can book, the Service must be active, connected to a **Book appointment** action, and backed by usable teammate availability.

<ol className="steps">
  <li>Confirm the Service is active.</li>
  <li>Open <strong>Workflow</strong>, select a <strong>Book appointment</strong> node, and choose which Services that action may use.</li>
  <li>Open <strong>Agent Setup</strong> and test a complete booking.</li>
</ol>

## Review bookings

The **Services** page lists your Services and booked appointments, including customer, assignee, appointment time, status, booking ID, and creation time. You can also manage the appointment from **Calendar**.
