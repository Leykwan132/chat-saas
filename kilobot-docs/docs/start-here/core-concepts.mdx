# Core concepts

These terms describe how KiloBot organizes your team, agents, and customer work.

## Workspace

A workspace is the home for your organization. It contains team members, agents, plan usage, roles, and permissions. A teammate's role determines what they can view or manage.

## AI agent

An agent is the customer-facing assistant you configure. It has a name, AI model, system prompt, communication style, knowledge sources, workflow, channels, and performance data. A workspace can contain one or more agents depending on its plan.

## Knowledge and behavior

- **[Knowledge Base](/build-your-agent/knowledge-base)** — trusted business facts from websites, files, text, and Q&A
- **[Agent Setup](/build-your-agent/agent-setup)** — role, model, tone, response length, emoji use, and reply mode
- **[Workflow](/automate/workflow-overview)** — explicit conditions and actions for important customer intents

## Channels, conversations, and contacts

A channel is where a customer reaches you: Website, WhatsApp, Instagram, or Messenger. Messages appear as conversations in **Inbox**. The person behind a conversation is a **Contact**, with identity, channel details, tags, lead temperature, and history.

## Services, availability, and bookings

A **Service** defines what a customer can book, its duration, required details, and teammate assignment. **Availability** defines when each teammate can receive appointments. KiloBot combines both when Workflow runs **Book appointment**. Confirmed appointments appear in **Calendar** and in the conversation.

## Workflow and automation

Workflow decides what happens after a message enters. Conditions route intent to actions such as sending a message or file, booking, escalating, or closing the conversation. Separate Workflow views configure appointment **Reminders** and no-reply **Follow-ups**.

## Credits and permissions

AI activity consumes workspace credits. Models, features, member limits, channel limits, and Knowledge Base storage depend on the active plan. Permissions determine what each teammate can access.
