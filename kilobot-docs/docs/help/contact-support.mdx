# Contact support

## Overview

Use Contact support when troubleshooting does not unblock you. Prefer the bug report form for reproducible product issues, WhatsApp for account or setup help, and email when you need a detailed written trail. Include workspace and agent context without sending passwords, access tokens, or unnecessary customer data.

## Report a bug

The bug report form gives the team reproducible steps and environment details.

<ol className="steps">
  <li>Open the <Link to="https://forms.gle/Hoo56T7Qj3yEBEeZ9">KiloBot bug report form</Link>.</li>
  <li>Include workspace and agent name, page or feature, approximate date and time with timezone, steps that reproduce the problem, expected and actual results, browser and device, and a screenshot or short recording with sensitive information hidden.</li>
</ol>

## Reach support on WhatsApp

Use WhatsApp for account, setup, or operational help.

<ol className="steps">
  <li>Start a conversation with <Link to="https://wa.me/60129499394">KiloBot support on WhatsApp</Link>.</li>
  <li>Describe the outcome you are trying to achieve and where you are blocked.</li>
</ol>

## Send a detailed email

Email is suitable when you need to include several steps, screenshots, or team context. Send detailed requests to [support@kilobot.app](mailto:support@kilobot.app).

## Prepare before you contact support

Checking common causes first often resolves the issue without waiting on a reply.

<ol className="steps">
  <li>Check <Link to="/help/troubleshooting">Troubleshooting</Link>.</li>
  <li>Confirm the correct agent is selected.</li>
  <li>Note whether the problem affects one teammate, one channel, or the entire workspace.</li>
  <li>If a provider such as Meta is still reviewing a template or synchronizing a connection, include the visible provider status.</li>
</ol>

:::warning
Never send passwords, API keys, access tokens, complete payment details, or private customer exports through the bug form, WhatsApp, or email.
:::

For sales or demo requests, use the [KiloBot contact page](https://kilobot.app/contact).
