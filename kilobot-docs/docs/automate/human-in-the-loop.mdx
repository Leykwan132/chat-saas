# Human in the loop

<DocVerified date="2026-07-30" />

Human escalation pauses AI replies and gives a teammate space to take over when judgment, safety, or personal support matters more than automation.

<DocOutcomes>

### By the end, you will

- Add a Human escalation action
- Describe the condition that should trigger it
- See the escalated conversation ready for a teammate

</DocOutcomes>

## 1. Add human escalation

Add **Human escalation** from the relevant Workflow connection. When the condition matches, the AI raises the escalation and pauses its replies for a teammate.

## 2. Describe the condition

Use a narrow Name and Detail so the AI can identify when a teammate should take over.

<DocExample title="Northstar Dental human support">

**Name:** Needs a teammate

**Detail:** Match when the customer asks for a human, reports an urgent or sensitive concern, makes a complaint, or needs help that the agent cannot provide confidently. Do not match ordinary questions that the Knowledge Base can answer.

</DocExample>
