# Human in the loop

<DocVerified date="2026-07-30" />

Human escalation pauses AI replies and gives a teammate space to take over when judgment, safety, or personal support matters more than automation.

<DocOutcomes>

### By the end, you will

- Recognize when a teammate should take over
- Pause AI replies with a clear escalation condition
- Close a conversation only when it is complete

</DocOutcomes>

## Decide when a person is needed

Escalate when:

- The customer directly asks to speak with a person.
- The agent cannot answer confidently from trusted knowledge.
- The request involves a complaint, exception, safety concern, or sensitive decision.
- Your team has promised that a person will review this type of request.

Do not use escalation as a substitute for missing business knowledge. Add stable facts to the Knowledge Base and reserve human involvement for requests that require judgment or ownership.

## Add a clear escalation

<DocExample title="Northstar Dental human support">

**Name:** Needs a teammate

**Detail:** Match when the customer asks for a human, reports an urgent or sensitive concern, makes a complaint, or needs help that the agent cannot provide confidently. Do not match ordinary questions that the Knowledge Base can answer.

</DocExample>

  <li>Enter a narrow Name and Detail.</li>

## Close a completed conversation

Use **Close conversation** only when a narrow condition proves that no more action is required. Closing is terminal, so do not connect it to broad signals such as a short reply or a general thank-you that may still need follow-up.
