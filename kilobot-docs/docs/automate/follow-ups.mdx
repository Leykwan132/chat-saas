# Follow-ups

Follow-ups re-engage eligible customers after they have not replied. A customer reply stops later attempts. Use Reminders when timing is tied to an appointment.

### By the end, you will

- Choose the follow-up audience and activation scope
- Configure timing, attempts, and approved messages
- Verify replies stop later attempts

<DocPrerequisites>

- A connected WhatsApp channel
- One or more **Approved** Message Templates
- A clearly defined audience
- A safe test Contact or controlled lead group

</DocPrerequisites>

## Choose the activation scope

| Scope | Includes | Best for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Future only** | Eligible messages that occur after activation | A clean first launch |
| **Current & future** | Eligible existing conversations and new messages | A reviewed backlog that should enter follow-up |

## Configure the sequence

<ol className="steps">
  <li>Open <strong>Workflows → Follow-ups</strong> and choose a scope.</li>
  <li>Select the eligible audience, such as the intended lead statuses.</li>
  <li>Set <strong>Start after</strong> to define the initial no-reply wait.</li>
  <li>Choose the maximum attempts.</li>
  <li>For multiple attempts, set the reattempt interval and choose whether attempts use the same or different templates.</li>
  <li>Select approved templates and choose <strong>Confirm</strong>.</li>
  <li>Review the Summary, then turn Follow-ups on.</li>
</ol>

## Monitor History

Open **History** from the Follow-up Summary to inspect scheduled, sent, failed, skipped, and cancelled activity. The table includes customer, template, scheduled time, sent time, status, and estimated Meta cost where pricing is known.

Meta template charges are separate from KiloBot credits. Estimates use known template rates; unknown categories are excluded instead of guessed.

<DocSuccess>

- The Summary matches the intended audience, delay, attempts, and templates
- Follow-ups are active
- A controlled no-reply test schedules the expected attempts
- A test reply prevents the next attempt

</DocSuccess>
