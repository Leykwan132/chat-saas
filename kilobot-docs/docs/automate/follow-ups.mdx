# Follow-ups

<DocVerified date="2026-07-28" />

Follow-ups re-engage eligible customers after they have not replied. A customer reply stops later attempts. Use Reminders when timing is tied to an appointment. Create and approve the templates for closed-window sends under [Message templates](/engage/message-templates).

<DocOutcomes>

### By the end, you will

- Choose the follow-up audience and activation scope
- Configure timing, attempts, and approved messages
- Stop later attempts when customers reply

</DocOutcomes>

## Smart follow-up and reminder

Use the Meta conversation window to choose the message type before each attempt. The window starts when the customer sends a message and stays open for 24 hours.

| Conversation window | Message path | Pricing impact |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Open** | Send a normal message | Avoids an unnecessary template charge |
| **Closed** | Send an approved WhatsApp Message Template | Meta's current template pricing applies |

This check lets smart follow-ups and smart reminders use a normal message while the customer is still within the window. Meta charges are separate from KiloBot credits. See the [official WhatsApp pricing](https://whatsappbusiness.com/products/platform-pricing/) for current rates and treat any in-app estimate as an estimate.

## Choose the activation scope

| Scope | Includes | Best for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Future only** | Eligible messages that occur after activation | A clean first launch |
| **Current & future** | Eligible existing conversations and new messages | A reviewed backlog that should enter follow-up |

## Configure the sequence

<ol className="steps">
  <li>Open <strong>Workflows → Follow-ups</strong> and choose a scope.</li>
  <li>Select the eligible audience, such as the intended lead statuses.</li>
  <li>Set <strong>Start after</strong> to define the initial no-reply wait.</li>
  <li>Choose the maximum attempts.</li>
  <li>For multiple attempts, set the reattempt interval and choose whether attempts use the same or different templates.</li>
  <li>Select approved templates and choose <strong>Confirm</strong>.</li>
  <li>Review the Summary, then turn Follow-ups on.</li>
</ol>

## Monitor History

Open **History** from the Follow-up Summary to inspect scheduled, sent, failed, skipped, and cancelled activity. The table includes customer, template, scheduled time, sent time, status, and estimated Meta cost where pricing is known.

Estimates use known template rates; unknown categories are excluded instead of guessed.
