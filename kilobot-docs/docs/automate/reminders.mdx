# Reminders

Reminders send an approved WhatsApp Message Template before a booked appointment. Use them for appointment-time messaging; use **Follow-ups** when the trigger is a customer who has not replied.

<DocPrerequisites>

- An active Service with bookable appointments
- A connected WhatsApp channel
- An **Approved** reminder Message Template
- Correct appointment customer phone numbers and timezones

</DocPrerequisites>

## Choose the activation scope

| Scope | Includes | Best for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Future only** | Eligible appointments created after activation | A clean, low-risk launch |
| **Current & future** | Remaining eligible sends for current upcoming appointments and new appointments | Applying the automation to an already-reviewed calendar |

The scope controls which appointments enter scheduling. It does not modify the appointments.

## Configure and activate

<ol className="steps">
  <li>Open <strong>Workflows → Reminders</strong>.</li>
  <li>Choose the activation scope.</li>
  <li>Choose how many reminders to send and when each should be sent.</li>
  <li>Select an approved WhatsApp template and choose <strong>Confirm</strong>.</li>
  <li>Review the Summary, then turn Reminders on.</li>
</ol>

<DocExample title="Northstar Consultation">

Send one reminder 24 hours before the appointment and a second reminder 2 hours before it. Start with **Future only** while verifying the workflow.

</DocExample>

## Monitor History

Open **History** from the Reminder Summary. Review the customer, template, scheduled time, sent time, status, and estimated Meta charge. Statuses include scheduled, sent, failed, skipped, and cancelled.

Meta charges for WhatsApp templates separately from KiloBot credits. KiloBot shows estimates only where the template category has a known rate; unknown categories are excluded instead of guessed.

<DocSuccess>

- The Summary shows the intended scope, timing, and approved template
- Reminders are active
- A controlled test booking produces the expected scheduled History rows
- Sent reminders appear in the customer's Inbox conversation

</DocSuccess>
