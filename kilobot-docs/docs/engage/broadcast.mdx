# Broadcast

<DocVerified date="2026-07-28" />

Broadcast sends one approved WhatsApp Message Template to a selected group of Contacts. Use it for a planned campaign or announcement, not one-to-one support.

<DocOutcomes>

### By the end, you will

- Choose an approved template and audience
- Review message variables, recipients, and estimated charges
- Send safely and inspect delivery history

</DocOutcomes>

## Create a Broadcast

<ol className="steps">
  <li>Open <strong>Broadcast → Create a broadcast</strong> and choose <strong>New Broadcast</strong>.</li>
  <li>Select the WhatsApp channel and an approved template.</li>
  <li>Define the audience using Contact criteria.</li>
  <li>Review the recipient count and preview resolved parameters.</li>
  <li>Review the estimated Meta charge.</li>
  <li>Send now or choose the available schedule option.</li>
  <li>Confirm only after checking the audience, template, and timing.</li>
</ol>

<DocExample title="Safe first campaign">

Create a small internal test audience, send the complete campaign to that group, and confirm the rendered variables and reply path before selecting production Contacts.

</DocExample>

## Review history

Open **Broadcast History**, select a Broadcast, and review its overview, recipients, and delivery status. Successfully sent messages also appear in the customer's **Inbox** thread and **Action History**, giving teammates context when the customer replies.

## Understand charges

Meta charges for delivered WhatsApp templates separately from KiloBot credits. The displayed MYR amount is an estimate based on known template pricing; final charges come from Meta. Unknown categories are excluded instead of guessed.

<DocSuccess>

- The correct audience received the intended approved template
- Template variables rendered correctly
- Recipient outcomes are visible in Broadcast History
- Replies can be continued from Inbox

</DocSuccess>

:::warning
Meta approval does not establish consent. Your organization remains responsible for appropriate audiences, useful content, frequency, and applicable messaging rules.
:::
