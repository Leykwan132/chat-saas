# Broadcast

Broadcast sends one approved WhatsApp Message Template to a selected group of Contacts.

Use it for a planned campaign or announcement, not for one-to-one support.

## Before you begin

Confirm that the agent has:

- A connected WhatsApp channel
- An **Approved** **Message Template**
- Enough eligible **Contacts**
- An audience with a legitimate reason to receive the message

Review Meta policy and expected template cost.

## Create a Broadcast

Avoid sending a test campaign to a production audience.

<ol className="steps">
  <li>Open <strong>Broadcast</strong> and choose the new Broadcast action.</li>
  <li>Select the WhatsApp channel and <strong>Approved</strong> template.</li>
  <li>Define the audience using available Contact criteria.</li>
  <li>Review the recipient count.</li>
  <li>Preview the template with its parameters.</li>
  <li>Use the <strong>Cost Calculator</strong> for the expected provider charge — see <Link to="/insights/usage-and-billing#separate-kilobot-credits-from-whatsapp-charges">Usage and billing</Link>.</li>
  <li>Choose the available send or schedule option.</li>
  <li>Confirm only after checking audience, template, and timing.</li>
</ol>

## Review history

History is paginated. Successfully sent Broadcast messages also appear in the customer's **Inbox** thread and **Action History**, which helps teammates understand later replies.

<ol className="steps">
  <li>Open <strong>Broadcast</strong> history.</li>
  <li>Select a Broadcast to review its overview, recipients, and status.</li>
</ol>

## Protect channel quality

Use specific audiences, useful content, and reasonable frequency. Monitor failures and customer replies. Repeated irrelevant sends can harm trust and may affect the connected WhatsApp account.
