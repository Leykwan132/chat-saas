# Message Templates

<DocVerified date="2026-07-28" />

Message Templates are Meta-approved WhatsApp messages. Create them before you configure a **Broadcast**, **Reminder**, or **Follow-up**.

<DocOutcomes>

### By the end, you will

- Create a complete WhatsApp Message Template
- Add examples and media where required
- Submit the template and confirm approval before use

</DocOutcomes>

<DocPrerequisites>

- A connected WhatsApp channel
- Final copy and a clear reason for contacting the customer
- Example values for every variable
- Header media ready when the template uses an image or document

</DocPrerequisites>

## Create a template

<ol className="steps">
  <li>Open <strong>Outreach → Templates</strong> and choose <strong>New template</strong>.</li>
  <li>Enter a lowercase template name, language, and the category that best matches the message.</li>
  <li>Write the body and add an example for every variable.</li>
  <li>Turn on <strong>Header</strong>, <strong>Buttons</strong>, or <strong>Footer</strong> only when needed.</li>
  <li>Preview the complete message, then submit it to Meta.</li>
</ol>

<DocExample title="Northstar appointment reminder">

**Body:** Hi `{{1}}`, this is a reminder for your `{{2}}` appointment on `{{3}}` at `{{4}}`. Reply here if you need help.

**Examples:** `Aisyah`, `Consultation`, `12 August`, `10:00 AM`

</DocExample>

## Understand the status

| Status | Meaning | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Submitting** | KiloBot is sending the request | Wait; do not submit a duplicate |
| **In review** | Meta is reviewing the template | Wait for the provider decision |
| **Approved** | The template can be selected | Use it in Outreach or Workflows |
| **Failed** | Submission or review failed | Open it, correct the issue, and resubmit |

Only **Approved** templates appear in sending selectors. Meta may later update a template category without removing its approved status.

## Understand charges

Meta charges for WhatsApp template delivery separately from KiloBot credits. The amount depends on Meta's current category, destination, and pricing. KiloBot may show an estimated MYR amount; treat it as an estimate and review the final provider charge in your Meta account.

<DocSuccess>

- The template status is **Approved**
- The preview contains the intended copy and resolved examples
- The template appears in the relevant Broadcast, Reminder, or Follow-up selector

</DocSuccess>

:::warning
Approval does not replace customer consent or local messaging requirements. Do not schedule a time-sensitive campaign around a template that is still **In review**.
:::
