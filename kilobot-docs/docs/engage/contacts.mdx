# Contacts

<DocVerified date="2026-07-28" />

## Confirm the identity

A Contact may originate from a phone number, Instagram identity, Messenger identity, Website visitor, or imported record. Search by the known channel identity before editing or importing another record.

## Use lead temperature consistently

| Temperature | Meaning | Northstar example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hot | Strong immediate intent | Requests the next available consultation |
| Warm | Meaningful interest that still needs progress | Asks about services and prices but has not booked |
| Cold | Not currently progressing | Declines next steps or has no current need |

Lead Temperature can affect Follow-up audiences, prioritization, assignment practice, and reporting. Update it when customer intent changes.

## Maintain useful context

<ol className="steps">
  <li>Keep the customer name and reachable contact information accurate.</li>
  <li>Use tags for durable segments or operating labels.</li>
  <li>Set Lead Temperature from current evidence.</li>
  <li>Open the linked Inbox conversation or booking when context is needed.</li>
</ol>

## Avoid duplicates

Search before importing or creating. Separate identities across platforms are not automatically proof that two records belong to one person. Do not treat records as interchangeable without confirmation. If no merge control is available, preserve both records and use careful tags or team process rather than overwriting identity.
