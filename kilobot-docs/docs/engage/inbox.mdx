# Inbox

<DocVerified date="2026-07-28" />

Inbox brings supported channel conversations into one operational view. Use it to find new work, manage AI and human ownership, reply, maintain customer context, and review actions that happened outside the visible message thread.

<DocOutcomes>

### By the end, you will

- Find conversations and understand the Inbox layout
- Manage AI and teammate ownership
- Reply, update customer context, and review completed actions

</DocOutcomes>

## Learn the layout

Inbox has three working areas: conversation list and filters, the selected message thread and composer, and customer/conversation details.

## Find the right conversation

| Filter | Use it for |
| --- | --- |
| Unread | New customer activity needing review |
| Assignee | Personal queue, teammate queue, or unassigned work |
| Platform | Channel-specific incidents or operating queues |
| Status | Active or resolved conversation states |
| Lead Temperature | Prioritizing Hot and Warm opportunities |
| Tags | Durable operational or campaign segments |

## Hand off between AI and a teammate

Human escalation pauses AI handling so a teammate does not compete with automated replies.

<ol className="steps">
  <li>Open the conversation and read the latest customer request.</li>
  <li>Pause or disable AI replies using the available conversation control.</li>
  <li>Assign the conversation to the responsible teammate.</li>
  <li>Send the human reply and add any useful tag or lead update.</li>
  <li>Resume AI only when the human-led work is complete and automation is appropriate again.</li>
</ol>

## Maintain conversation context

Use details to change the assignee, add or remove tags, set Lead Temperature, and review the linked [Contact](/engage/contacts). Attachments, reactions, read state, and delivery information appear when the connected platform supports them. Growth and Business can understand supported customer-sent audio and photos; audio transcripts may appear beneath audio while image understanding remains part of AI processing.

## Work with bookings

Choose **Create booking** when the Contact and Service are known. The most recently updated booking and prior booking activity appear in details. Appointment status remains consistent with [Calendar](/bookings/calendar).

## Review Action History

Action History provides operational context for assignment changes, AI-state changes, tags, lead status, Broadcasts, Reminders, Follow-ups, and appointment activity. Use it when the message text does not explain why a state changed.

## Daily operator checklist

1. Review unread and unassigned conversations.
2. Confirm urgent or escalated conversations have an owner.
3. Reply or record the next action.
4. Update lead temperature and durable tags.
5. Check failed outbound activity or booking changes.
6. Resolve or hand over work before ending the shift.
