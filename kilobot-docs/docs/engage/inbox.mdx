# Inbox

Inbox brings customer conversations from every connected channel into one team workspace.

## Find the right conversation

Unread counts help the team identify new activity. Use filters to narrow the list before opening a thread — by assignment, platform, status, lead temperature, or tags.

<ol className="steps">
  <li>Open <strong>Inbox</strong> for the selected agent.</li>
  <li>Apply the filters you need.</li>
  <li>Select a conversation to open its message thread and customer details.</li>
</ol>

## Reply and manage AI behavior

Human escalation pauses AI handling so a teammate can work safely without competing automated messages. The thread distinguishes customer, teammate, AI, Workflow Reminder, and Follow-up activity. Attachments, reactions, read state, and delivery information appear when supported by the channel.

<ol className="steps">
  <li>Write a message in the reply composer to respond as a teammate.</li>
  <li>Enable or disable AI replies for the conversation, depending on permissions and routing configuration.</li>
</ol>

## Keep the customer record current

Lead Temperature values are defined in [Contacts](/engage/contacts).

<ol className="steps">
  <li>Open conversation details.</li>
  <li>Change the assignee, add or remove tags, or update <strong>Lead Temperature</strong> if permitted.</li>
  <li>Review <strong>Contact</strong> information.</li>
</ol>

## Work with bookings

Appointment statuses match those in [Calendar](/bookings/calendar).

<ol className="steps">
  <li>Choose <strong>Create booking</strong> from the conversation when the customer and Service are known.</li>
  <li>Review the most recently updated booking and booking history in the details panel.</li>
</ol>

## Review Action History

Action History records assignment changes, AI reply changes, tags, lead status, broadcasts, follow-ups, and appointment activity. Use it to understand what happened outside the visible message text.

<ol className="steps">
  <li>Open <strong>Action History</strong> in the conversation.</li>
</ol>
