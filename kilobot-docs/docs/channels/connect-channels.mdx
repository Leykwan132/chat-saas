# Channels overview

<DocVerified date="2026-07-28" />

Channels bring Website, WhatsApp, Instagram, and Messenger conversations into the selected agent’s [Inbox](/engage/inbox). Free supports one platform per agent. Paid plans support all supported platforms for every agent, subject to provider access.

<DocOutcomes>

### By the end, you will

- Choose the right channel for your customers
- Understand each connection status
- Connect a channel and verify a real conversation

</DocOutcomes>

## Understand the channel cards

KiloBot always shows WhatsApp, Instagram, and Messenger cards. A disconnected card says **Not connected** and provides **Connect**. Connected cards show the account identity and status.

| State | Meaning | Next action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Not connected | No active platform connection | Choose Connect |
| Pending | Authorization or setup has started | Finish the provider flow |
| Synchronizing | Initial supported data is being imported | Keep the connection active and wait |
| Connected | New supported messages can enter Inbox | Run inbound and outbound tests |
| Failed or disconnected | Authorization or provider access is incomplete | Review the platform guide before reconnecting |

## Verify every connection

<ol className="steps">
  <li>Send a new message from the customer side.</li>
  <li>Open <strong>Inbox</strong> for the selected agent.</li>
  <li>Confirm the message has the correct platform and Contact.</li>
  <li>Send a teammate reply.</li>
  <li>Confirm the reply reaches the customer-side account.</li>
</ol>

<DocSuccess>

- The card shows Connected.
- A new customer-side message appears in the correct Inbox.
- A teammate reply reaches the customer.

</DocSuccess>
