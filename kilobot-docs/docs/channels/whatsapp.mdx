# WhatsApp

<DocVerified date="2026-07-28" />

WhatsApp connects through Meta’s authorization flow. Setup may remain pending while account access, number registration, or initial synchronization completes.

<DocOutcomes>

### By the end, you will

- Complete the Meta connection flow
- Confirm synchronization finishes successfully
- See incoming messages, teammate replies, and safe disconnection

</DocOutcomes>

## Conversation window

- **When it opens:** A customer messages your WhatsApp Business number.
- **While it is open:** KiloBot can send service replies.
- **After it closes:** WhatsApp generally requires an approved template to start a new business-initiated conversation.

## Connect with Coexistence

Coexistence lets you use the same WhatsApp Business number in both KiloBot and the WhatsApp Business app.

<ol className="steps">
  <li>Open <strong>Channels</strong> and choose <strong>Connect</strong> on WhatsApp.</li>
  <li>Complete Meta sign-in without closing the authorization window early.</li>
  <li>Select the intended business and WhatsApp account.</li>
  <li>Choose <strong>WhatsApp Business app</strong>, then enter your phone number.</li>
</ol>

<div className="docs-image-compact">

![Connect WhatsApp with coexistence](https://storage.kilobot.app/docs/docs-coexistence.png)

</div>

<ol className="steps" start={5}>
  <li>Grant the requested messaging access.</li>
  <li>When Meta offers <strong>coexistence</strong>, select it to keep using the WhatsApp Business app on this number, then complete the in-product prompts.</li>
  <li>Scan the QR code from the WhatsApp Business app on your phone to confirm the connection.</li>
  <li>Approve the prompt to share your own WhatsApp chats with KiloBot.</li>
  <li>Add a payment method if Meta asks for one, then complete the remaining prompts.</li>
  <li>Return to KiloBot to continue the initial history sync.</li>
</ol>

![WhatsApp connection message and QR permission](https://storage.kilobot.app/docs/connect-msg.png)

## Disconnect

Disconnecting stops KiloBot from sending and receiving WhatsApp messages for this channel and clears its authorization. It does not delete the WhatsApp Business account or the app from your phone.

<ol className="steps">
  <li>Open <strong>Channels</strong> and select WhatsApp.</li>
  <li>Choose <strong>Disconnect</strong>.</li>
  <li>Review the change, then confirm.</li>
  <li>Check that the channel card shows <strong>Disconnected</strong> before connecting another number or provider.</li>
</ol>

If the number uses coexistence, complete any Meta prompts shown during the disconnect or later reconnect flow.

## Before reconnecting

Confirm that administrator access still exists and that the intended business, account, and number have not changed. Reconnecting can repeat authorization and synchronization; avoid starting multiple connection attempts.
