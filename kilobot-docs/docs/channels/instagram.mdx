# Instagram

<DocVerified date="2026-07-28" />

Connect a professional Instagram account so supported direct messages enter the selected agent’s Inbox.

### By the end, you will

- Connect the correct professional Instagram account
- Verify a direct message reaches Inbox
- Send a teammate reply from KiloBot

<DocPrerequisites>

- A professional Instagram account connected to the appropriate Meta business
- Administrator access to the related business assets
- KiloBot channel-management permission

</DocPrerequisites>

## Connect and verify

<ol className="steps">
  <li>Open <strong>Channels</strong> and choose <strong>Connect</strong> on Instagram.</li>
  <li>Sign in to Meta and select the intended professional account.</li>
  <li>Grant the requested messaging access and return to KiloBot.</li>
  <li>Send a new direct message from a separate Instagram account.</li>
  <li>Confirm the message in Inbox and send a teammate reply.</li>
</ol>
