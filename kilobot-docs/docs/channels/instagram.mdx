# Instagram

<DocVerified date="2026-07-28" />

Connect a professional Instagram account so supported direct messages enter the selected agent’s Inbox. Setup is straightforward: sign in, choose the account, and grant access.

<DocOutcomes>

### By the end, you will

- Connect the correct professional Instagram account
- See a direct message reach Inbox
- Send a teammate reply from KiloBot

</DocOutcomes>

:::important
Before connecting, make sure the professional Instagram account is connected to Meta Business Suite and that you have administrator access.
:::

## Conversation window

- **When it opens:** A customer messages your professional Instagram account.
- **While it is open:** KiloBot can reply.
- **After it closes:** Instagram rules may limit business-initiated messages or require the customer to message again.

## Connect

<ol className="steps">
  <li>Open <strong>Channels</strong> and choose <strong>Connect</strong> on Instagram.</li>
  <li>Sign in to Meta, select the intended professional account, and grant messaging access.</li>
  <li>Return to KiloBot, then send a new direct message and reply from Inbox.</li>
</ol>

If no eligible account appears, confirm professional-account status, business linkage, and your Meta administrator access before retrying.
