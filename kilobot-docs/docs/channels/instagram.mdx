# Instagram

<DocVerified date="2026-07-28" />

Connect a professional Instagram account so supported direct messages enter the selected agent’s Inbox. Setup is straightforward: sign in, choose the account, and grant access.

<DocOutcomes>

### By the end, you will

- Connect the correct professional Instagram account
- Verify a direct message reaches Inbox
- Send a teammate reply from KiloBot

</DocOutcomes>

## Conversation window

When a customer messages your professional Instagram account, Meta opens a conversation window. KiloBot can reply while it is open. After it closes, Instagram rules may limit business-initiated messages or require the customer to message again.

## Connect

<ol className="steps">
  <li>Open <strong>Channels</strong> and choose <strong>Connect</strong> on Instagram.</li>
  <li>Sign in to Meta, select the intended professional account, and grant messaging access.</li>
  <li>Return to KiloBot, then send a new direct message and reply from Inbox.</li>
</ol>
