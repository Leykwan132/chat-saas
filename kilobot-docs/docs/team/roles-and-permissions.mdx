# Roles and permissions

<DocVerified date="2026-07-28" />

Roles group permissions that control what a teammate can view or manage. Navigation and actions are permission-aware, so two teammates may see different screens in the same workspace.

<DocOutcomes>

### By the end, you will

- Match permissions to a teammate’s responsibilities
- Assign the smallest suitable access
- Test what the teammate can view and manage

</DocOutcomes>

## Start with responsibility

| Responsibility | Likely access | Usually unnecessary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Answer customers | Conversations and Contacts | Agent configuration and team administration |
| Manage appointments | Conversations, Services, Availability, Calendar | Channels and team administration |
| Configure the customer experience | Agent Setup, Knowledge Base, Channels, Workflows | Team administration unless they also manage people |
| Administer the workspace | Teams and required organization settings | Daily conversation ownership unless operationally needed |

Use built-in roles where they fit. When custom roles are available, name them after responsibility, such as **Inbox operator** or **Booking manager**, rather than after a person.

<DocExample title="Northstar receptionist">

The receptionist needs Conversations, Contacts, Services, and Calendar. They do not need to reconnect channels, change the agent prompt, or manage team membership.

</DocExample>

## Assign and test a role

<ol className="steps">
  <li>Open <strong>Settings → Teams</strong> and select the teammate.</li>
  <li>Choose the role that covers the defined responsibility.</li>
  <li>Save the change.</li>
  <li>Ask the teammate to open every required page and perform one representative action.</li>
  <li>Confirm sensitive controls are absent or disabled.</li>
</ol>
