# Lead Assignment

<DocVerified date="2026-07-28" />

Lead Assignment determines who owns a customer conversation. Clear ownership prevents duplicate replies and makes Inbox filters useful.

<DocOutcomes>

### By the end, you will

- Choose a conversation assignment strategy
- Configure teammate eligibility and booking ownership
- Verify new work reaches the intended teammate

</DocOutcomes>

## Choose a strategy

| Strategy | Use when | Watch for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Manual** | A coordinator decides ownership or volume is low | Unassigned conversations waiting too long |
| **Balanced** | New work should consider current distribution | Ineligible or unavailable teammates |
| **Round robin** | Eligible teammates should receive turns in sequence | Schedule differences and temporary absence |
| **Specific teammate** | One person owns a Service or specialist journey | Coverage when that person is unavailable |

Your plan and permissions determine which controls are available.

## Configure assignment

<ol className="steps">
  <li>Open <strong>Teams → Lead Assignment</strong> for the agent.</li>
  <li>Select the eligible teammates.</li>
  <li>Choose the routing strategy and save it.</li>
  <li>Send controlled test enquiries and verify ownership in Conversations.</li>
</ol>

For Bookings, each Service can prefer the conversation owner, use balanced or round-robin assignment, or select a specific teammate. The chosen teammate must also be available for the slot.

<DocExample title="Northstar front desk">

Northstar uses balanced assignment for general enquiries across two receptionists. The orthodontic consultation Service uses a specific specialist with dedicated Availability.

</DocExample>
