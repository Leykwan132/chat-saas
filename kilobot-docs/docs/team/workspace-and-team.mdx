# Workspace and team

<DocVerified date="2026-07-28" />

Use **Teams** for workspace membership, invitations, and roles. Configure the customer experience inside each agent.

<DocOutcomes>

### By the end, you will

- Invite a teammate to the correct workspace
- Assign the smallest suitable role
- Review, change, or remove team access

</DocOutcomes>

<DocPrerequisites>

- Owner or team-administration permission
- The teammate's correct email address
- A decided responsibility and matching role

</DocPrerequisites>

## Invite a teammate

<ol className="steps">
  <li>Open <strong>Teams</strong> or <strong>Settings → Teams</strong>.</li>
  <li>Choose <strong>Invite teammate</strong>.</li>
  <li>Enter the teammate's email and select the smallest suitable role.</li>
  <li>Send the invitation.</li>
  <li>Confirm the invitation becomes an active membership after acceptance.</li>
</ol>

If an invitation remains pending, verify the email and ask the recipient to check the same account before sending another.

## Maintain membership

Review members regularly. Update a role when responsibility changes, remove access when someone leaves, and reduce temporary administrative access after setup or incident work is complete.

| Situation | Action |
| --- | --- |
| Teammate cannot open a required page | Verify membership, selected workspace, and role |
| Teammate can change sensitive configuration unnecessarily | Assign a narrower role |
| Invitation went to the wrong email | Revoke it and invite the correct account |
| Teammate has left the organization | Remove membership promptly |

<DocSuccess>

- Every teammate uses an individual account
- Active members have the minimum access needed
- Pending invitations are intentional and sent to the correct email
- Departed teammates no longer have workspace access

</DocSuccess>
