# Privacy and Terms Data Security and Retention Design

## Goal

Update KiloBot's public Privacy Policy and Terms of Service with aligned, factual commitments about data security and retention.

## Scope

- Add a `Data security` section to the Privacy Policy using the user-provided security wording.
- Add a `Security and performance` section that identifies Cloudflare Workers as the website and web-application hosting provider and Cloudflare R2 as the media storage and delivery provider.
- Explain that Cloudflare processes technical request information, such as IP address, browser, operating system, and request data, to host, secure, and deliver the Services and stored media.
- Add an `Authentication service` section that identifies WorkOS AuthKit as KiloBot's authentication provider and links to WorkOS's privacy policy.
- State that KiloBot collects account identity information supplied through WorkOS, including first and last name, email address, profile details, and account preferences; WorkOS manages authentication credentials, and KiloBot does not store user passwords.
- Add a `Google sign-in` subsection that explains users may choose Google to create or access an account, are redirected to Google to authenticate, and receive the same KiloBot account experience as other sign-in methods.
- State that Google provides KiloBot only the user's name and email address for account creation and identification, never the user's Google password; link to Google's account-permissions and privacy pages.
- Add a `Payment service` section that identifies Stripe as the payment processor, explains that customers provide payment information directly to Stripe, and links to Stripe's privacy policy.
- State that Stripe may process identity, billing, payment, and transaction information to process payments, prevent fraud, and meet legal obligations, while KiloBot receives billing metadata and does not store full card numbers.
- Replace the existing abbreviated billing language with a `Subscription terms` section that explains plan descriptions, availability, prices, billing intervals, and applicable taxes are shown before checkout and may change for future subscription periods with reasonable advance notice.
- State that submitting an order creates an obligation to pay the displayed subscription fees, taxes, and disclosed charges, and that Stripe processes payments and recurring charges.
- State that paid subscriptions are billed in advance and automatically renew for the selected monthly or annual period through the payment method on file until cancelled through Stripe's customer billing portal or by contacting KiloBot before renewal; cancellation takes effect at the end of the current paid period.
- State that a failed or refused payment may result in suspended access until the outstanding amount is paid.
- State that KiloBot does not offer free trials. The Early Adopter Program is a separate, limited beta program for accepted participants that provides the Growth plan free for three months in exchange for feedback; it is not a free trial and does not automatically create a paid subscription unless KiloBot explicitly states otherwise.
- State that subscription and other paid fees are non-refundable except where required by applicable law, including no refund or credit for partial or unused billing periods.
- State that KiloBot may offer discounts through Stripe coupon or promotion codes, subject to the displayed eligibility, value, duration, and other terms; coupons are non-transferable, cannot be combined unless stated otherwise, and do not establish a future right to a discount.
- State that third-party charges, such as messaging-platform fees, are separate from KiloBot fees and remain the user's responsibility when applicable.
- Replace the current retention wording with an immediate-deletion standard: KiloBot deletes personal information and Service data when it is no longer needed for its original purpose.
- Preserve narrow exceptions for legal obligations, disputes, agreement enforcement, and service security/integrity.
- Add a `What KiloBot means` definition to the Terms covering KiloBot's website and subdomains, web application, APIs, integrations and widgets, related software or scripts, and documentation that KiloBot makes available.
- Expand the Terms' intellectual-property protections into a `Content provided by KiloBot` clause: content other than user content is owned by KiloBot or its licensors; all rights are reserved; use is limited to what is necessary to use the Services; and users may not copy, download, share, modify, translate, publish, sell, sublicense, transfer, or create derivative works without permission, subject to applicable statutory exceptions.
- Permit specifically identified content to be downloaded, copied, or shared only for its stated permitted purpose and with required attributions; direct intellectual-property complaints to KiloBot's listed contact information.
- Add a `Software license` Terms section: KiloBot and its licensors retain all intellectual, industrial, and other exclusive rights in the software and technical applications embedded in or related to the Services.
- Grant compliant users only a revocable, non-exclusive, non-sublicensable, non-transferable license to use that software and technical means solely within the Services' scope and purpose; exclude source-code access, use, and disclosure; reserve related techniques, algorithms, procedures, and documentation; and end all license rights when the agreement terminates or expires.
- Add a `Service reselling` Terms section that prohibits reproducing, duplicating, copying, selling, reselling, or exploiting any part of KiloBot or the Services without KiloBot's express prior written permission, including any permission issued through an authorised reseller programme.
- Expand the Terms' `Your content` section: users confirm they have the necessary rights and do not violate law or third-party rights; grant KiloBot a worldwide, non-exclusive, fully paid-up, royalty-free license to host, store, process, display, and transmit content only to operate, maintain, improve, and secure the Services; and, to the extent permitted by law, waive moral rights in submitted content.
- State that users are solely responsible for submitted content and KiloBot is not obligated to review, pre-screen, or moderate it. Permit KiloBot to remove, block, modify, or restrict access to content or accounts without notice following a complaint, infringement notice, public-authority order, or identified risk to users, third parties, or the Services.
- State that these content actions do not create compensation, damages, or reimbursement rights to the extent permitted by law.
- Expand the existing `Indemnification` clause: users must defend, indemnify, and hold harmless KiloBot, its affiliates, officers, directors, agents, partners, suppliers, and employees against claims, demands, damages, obligations, losses, liabilities, costs, debts, and reasonable legal fees arising from their use of the Services; transmitted or received data/content; Terms breaches; representations or warranty breaches; legal or third-party rights violations; account-submitted content (including unauthorised access using their credentials); wilful misconduct; and the acts or omissions of their related personnel, to the extent permitted by law.
- Add a `Changes to these Terms` section that permits KiloBot to amend the Terms and make reasonable efforts to give advance notice before material changes take effect, with materiality determined at KiloBot's discretion subject to applicable law.
- State that changes apply prospectively; continued use after their effective date accepts the revised Terms; users who do not accept must stop using the Services and either party may terminate the agreement where permitted; and a prior version governs the relationship before the revised Terms take effect and is available from KiloBot on request.
- Add a `Content backups` Terms section that explains KiloBot may perform backups but does not guarantee that Content will be free from loss or corruption or that a backup can be restored to a usable state.
- State that KiloBot will investigate known backup issues but, to the extent permitted by law, is not liable for Content integrity or an unsuccessful restoration; users must maintain complete, accurate, independent copies of their Content.
- Add a `Service interruption and availability` Terms section allowing KiloBot to interrupt or change the Services for maintenance, system updates, or other operational changes, with appropriate notice where reasonably practicable.
- State that KiloBot may suspend or discontinue the Services within the limits of law and, if discontinued, will reasonably cooperate to enable users to withdraw their personal data or information where required by applicable law.
- State that the Services may be unavailable for circumstances outside KiloBot's reasonable control, including force majeure events, labour actions, infrastructure failures, and blackouts; this is not an uptime or service-level commitment.
- Expand the Terms' warranty disclaimer: the Services are provided "as is" and "as available" at the user's own risk, without express, implied, statutory, or other warranties, including merchantability, fitness for a particular purpose, and non-infringement, and no advice or information creates an unstated warranty.
- State that KiloBot and its related parties do not guarantee accurate or reliable content, satisfaction of user requirements, availability at a particular time/location or on a particular browser, device, or operating system, uninterrupted/secure operation, error correction, or freedom from viruses or harmful components; downloaded content is at the user's risk.
- Disclaim warranties, endorsements, and responsibility for third-party offers, linked sites, services, and transactions, and preserve the mandatory-law exceptions for warranties and rights that cannot lawfully be disclaimed.
- Expand `Disclaimers and limitation of liability` so, to the maximum extent permitted by law, KiloBot and its affiliates, officers, directors, agents, partners, suppliers, and employees exclude liability for indirect, punitive, incidental, special, consequential, or exemplary losses, including lost profits, goodwill, use, data, and intangible losses.
- Include exclusions for losses from unauthorised access, hacking or tampering; content errors or omissions; personal injury or property damage; server or stored-information access; interruption of transmission; malware; reliance on content; and unlawful, offensive, or defamatory acts of users or third parties.
- Replace the existing MYR 100 unpaid-user cap with an aggregate liability cap equal to the fees the user paid to KiloBot during the twelve months before the claim or the shorter duration of the agreement, and apply the clause regardless of the asserted legal theory, even if KiloBot was advised of possible damage.
- Preserve mandatory-law and jurisdiction-specific exceptions, including rights that cannot lawfully be excluded or limited.
- Add a matching `Data security` section to the Terms. It will describe reasonable technical and organizational safeguards, user responsibility for credentials and incident reporting, and the absence of an absolute-security guarantee.
- State in the Terms that users may request account termination and stop using the Services at any time by contacting KiloBot through the contact information in the Terms.
- State in the Terms that data may be deleted when it is no longer required to provide the Services, subject to the stated retention exceptions.
- Permit KiloBot to suspend or delete inappropriate, offensive, or Terms-violating accounts at its discretion and without notice; state that this does not create a compensation, damages, or reimbursement right to the extent permitted by law, and does not excuse outstanding fees.
- Rename the homepage footer's `Terms` link to `Terms of Service` without changing its route or layout.
- Update both legal documents' displayed last-updated date to August 03, 2026.

## Non-goals

- No changes to product behavior, data-deletion systems, vendor contracts, or retention automation.
- No GDPR-specific claim or fixed server-log retention period.
- No copied Webflow or JsDelivr disclosures, data-transfer certification claims, cookies, or Web Application Firewall commitment.
- No claim that KiloBot performs credit checks or that Stripe privacy requests replace users' rights with KiloBot.
- No claim that KiloBot stores user passwords.
- No GDPR or UK GDPR legal-basis language in the Google sign-in disclosure.
- No free-trial offer, automatic conversion of an Early Adopter Program benefit into a paid subscription, late-payment penalty, overage-charge, or fixed coupon-duration commitment.
- No promise that a reseller programme currently exists.
- No new uptime, breach-notification, or absolute-security commitment.

## Document Structure

`src/content/privacyPolicy.tsx` will separate retention and security into distinct, easy-to-find sections. The retention section applies to personal information and Service data; the data-security section contains the supplied commitment and retains the existing limitation that no method of transmission or storage is completely secure. An `Authentication service` section will identify WorkOS AuthKit, define the account information KiloBot receives, clarify that WorkOS manages authentication credentials, and link to WorkOS's privacy policy. Its `Google sign-in` subsection will describe the Google redirect and the name/email data shared with KiloBot, state that KiloBot never receives a Google password, and link to Google's account-permissions and privacy pages. A `Security and performance` section will identify Cloudflare Workers and Cloudflare R2, describe their limited technical-data processing role, and link to Cloudflare's privacy policy. A `Payment service` section will identify Stripe, distinguish data Stripe collects directly from KiloBot's limited billing metadata, state payment-processing/fraud-prevention purposes, and link to Stripe's privacy policy.

`src/content/termsOfService.tsx` will add a `What KiloBot means` section after the agreement, defining the website, subdomains, web application, APIs, integrations/widgets, distributed software or scripts, and documentation that comprise KiloBot. A `Changes to these Terms` section will expand the existing last-updated sentence: KiloBot may change the Terms, will use reasonable efforts to give advance notice of material changes, applies changes prospectively, treats continued post-effective-date use as acceptance, permits service discontinuation if a user does not accept, and provides prior Terms versions on request. Its subscription section will become `Subscription terms` and cover checkout disclosures, price/plan changes for future periods, the payment obligation created by an order, Stripe payment processing, automatic monthly or annual renewal, portal/contact cancellation before renewal, end-of-period cancellation, payment-failure suspension, and third-party messaging fees. It will expressly state that KiloBot has no free trial. The Early Adopter Program will be described as a distinct, accepted-participant beta benefit that waives Growth-plan fees for three months in exchange for feedback and does not automatically become a paid subscription. The subscription section will state the no-refund policy subject to mandatory law and define coupon/promotion-code discounts as limited to their displayed terms. Its existing intellectual-property section will become `Content provided by KiloBot`, reserving all rights in KiloBot or licensor content other than user content, allowing use only as necessary to use the Services, defining prohibited reuse, preserving statutory exceptions, and routing rights complaints to KiloBot's contact details. A dedicated `Software license` section will retain KiloBot's and its licensors' exclusive rights in embedded or related software and technical applications, grant compliant users only a revocable non-exclusive, non-sublicensable, non-transferable license for the Services' limited purpose, exclude source-code rights, reserve technical methods and documentation, and end the license at agreement termination or expiry. A `Service reselling` section will separately prohibit reproducing, duplicating, copying, selling, reselling, or exploiting the Services absent express prior written permission from KiloBot, including permission under an authorised reseller programme, without claiming such a programme currently exists. The `Your content` section will retain customer ownership while defining legal-rights assurances, KiloBot's operational license, conditional moral-rights waiver, user responsibility, no general review/moderation obligation, and content removal/access restrictions following complaints, notices, orders, or risk. `Indemnification` will retain its existing coverage and extend to every listed account/use, breach, third-party right, legal violation, account-content, credential-access, and wilful-misconduct scenario, as well as the user’s related personnel, and apply to defence costs, claims, damages, liabilities, losses, debts, and expenses to the extent permitted by law. The current short liability section will become `Disclaimers and limitation of liability`, with a `Disclaimer of warranties` subsection that provides the as-is/as-available, own-risk, no-warranty, non-guarantee, downloaded-content, third-party service, device/browser, and mandatory-law language. It will also list the requested direct and indirect loss exclusions, a cap equal to fees paid in the shorter of the preceding twelve months or agreement duration, all legal-theory coverage, and mandatory-law/carve-out language; it will not retain the existing MYR 100 cap. A `Content backups` section after `Your content` will avoid promising a backup frequency or recovery level: KiloBot may create backups and will investigate known problems, but does not guarantee loss-free or usable restoration, limits liability to the extent permitted by law, and requires users to retain independent copies. A `Service interruption and availability` section will permit necessary maintenance, updates, and operational changes with appropriate notice when reasonably practicable; reserve the right to suspend or discontinue the Services within legal limits; state KiloBot's reasonable cooperation to enable lawful data withdrawal if the Services are discontinued; and explain that external events such as force majeure, labour actions, infrastructure breakdowns, or blackouts can make the Services unavailable. It will also add a focused data-security section near account obligations. That section will point users to protect credentials, notify KiloBot about suspected unauthorized access, and clarify that safeguards do not guarantee absolute security. An explicit `Account termination` section will state that users may request account closure by contacting KiloBot and will include the corresponding data-deletion statement. A separate `Suspension and termination by KiloBot` section will allow KiloBot to suspend or delete inappropriate, offensive, or Terms-violating accounts without notice, preserve the existing enforcement and surviving-obligations terms, exclude compensation to the extent permitted by law, and retain responsibility for outstanding fees.
