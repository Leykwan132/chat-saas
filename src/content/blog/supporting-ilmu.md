Today we are supporting Ilmu Mini v3.3 from YTL AI Labs in Kilobot.

Ilmu Mini understands Malaysian culture better: local language, how people talk, and what feels natural here. It is also fast, so replies stay quick in the inbox.

## Available on the Free plan

Ilmu Mini v3.3 is available on Kilobot's Free plan. Anyone can use it without paying.

## Why Ilmu for Kilobot

Most AI models are trained for a global audience. Malaysian customers do not talk that way. They mix languages, use local phrasing, and expect replies that feel natural here.

Ilmu Mini is built to understand that Malaysian context. Supporting it means Kilobot agents can meet customers where they are, with replies that sound local instead of imported.

YTL AI Labs also runs very reliable inference, so agents stay responsive when conversations pick up.
